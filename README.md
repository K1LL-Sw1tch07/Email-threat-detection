# 🛡️ Email Threat Detection & Forensics Platform

An AI-powered email forensic analysis platform designed to detect, investigate, and explain malicious and suspicious emails.

The platform analyzes `.eml` files and combines **email header forensics, authentication analysis, relay-path reconstruction, URL intelligence, reputation checks, social-engineering detection, deterministic threat scoring, and AI-assisted investigation** into a single dashboard.

> 🚀 Built as an SIH (Smart India Hackathon) MVP for email threat detection and forensic investigation.

---

## 📌 Problem

Email remains one of the most widely used communication channels across:

- Government
- Banking
- Education
- Enterprises
- Critical infrastructure

It is also a major attack vector for:

- Phishing
- Credential theft
- Business Email Compromise (BEC)
- Sender impersonation
- Malware delivery
- Financial fraud
- Social engineering

Traditional email analysis often requires manually inspecting headers, authentication results, URLs, and relay information.

This project aims to provide an **automated and explainable email investigation workflow**.
